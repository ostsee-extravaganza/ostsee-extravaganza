Fotos hinzufügen

Drop files into photos/YYYY-MM-DD/ and run python3 tools/fotos.py. It makes the web copies and thumbnails, strips EXIF from them, reads the capture time for ordering, and rebuilds the manifest. Dated folders land on their trip day automatically; photos/planung/ becomes its own group.
